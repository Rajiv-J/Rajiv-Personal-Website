Building technology to help you move.

Hi, my name is Rajiv, and I'm an engineer who enjoys learning how to build systems that assist people and make an impact on their lives. I recently completed my M.S. in Mechanical Engineering at Carnegie Mellon, advised by Inseung Kang at the MetaMobility Lab. Before that, I earned two B.S. degrees at Virginia Tech, in Mechanical Engineering and in Robotics and Mechatronics. Over time, I've developed a full-stack mechatronics background by designing hardware, integrating embedded systems, and deploying intelligent controllers.

Currently, I'm focused on three questions:

  1. How can we build a full-stack, open-source platform for exoskeleton research groups?
  2. How can we make wearable exoskeletons comfortable for patient populations to wear?
  3. Can a digital twin, trained with reinforcement learning, respond the way a real person would, so we can train controllers without collecting real-world data?
